AI Business Strategy & Transformation Whiteboard
================================================

FILES
- AI-Business-Strategy-Transformation-Whiteboard.html — interactive offline whiteboard.
- AI-Business-Strategy-Transformation-Whiteboard.svg — 6200 × 3900 scalable master canvas.
- AI-Business-Strategy-Transformation-Whiteboard.pdf — printable one-page overview.
- VERIFICATION.txt — executed checks, visual review, and limitations.
- source/ — reproducible Python generator and verification scripts.

OPEN AND USE
1. Extract the ZIP before use.
2. Open the HTML file in a current desktop browser. No server, account, or network connection is needed.
3. Drag the canvas to pan; use wheel/pinch or +/− to zoom; click a P/T phase card to open its detail panel. Fit board (or keyboard 0) resets the view. Arrow keys pan.
4. Open the SVG directly or import it into a whiteboard/presentation tool. Because it is vector-based, text and shapes remain sharp while zooming.
5. Print the PDF in landscape orientation using “fit to printable area”. The PDF is a big-picture overview; use HTML/SVG for drill-down.

READING ORDER
North Star → transformation question → paired P/T phase tracks → cross-cutting rails → evidence zone. Apply GO / ADAPT / HOLD / STOP at every phase. The management panel lists evidence limits, decision owners, and open decisions.

IMPORTANT BOUNDARY
This is an internal planning aid. It does not claim legal, privacy, security, labour, technical, production, or scale approval. Reviews are required and no approval is assumed. No company facts, customer data, or quantitative outcome claims are included.

PORTABILITY & PRIVACY
All HTML styles, scripts, and content are embedded. There are no external CDNs, analytics, trackers, network calls, or third-party accounts.
